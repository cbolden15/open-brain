{"schema_version":1,"workflow_id":"guide:synthetic-1","expected_revision":0,"action":"start","confirmation":null,"arguments":{}}

Expected human output visibly escapes controls while preserving all readable text/newline. It must never emit ESC/BEL/bidi controls or mutate the engine-approved projection.

Apply the three crash positions to every row with actual persistence; request-less configure/preview/setup/policy use halt oracle.
